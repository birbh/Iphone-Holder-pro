# Iphone-Holder-pro

- This is the holder/stand for iphone 12 and newer which can hold phone with magsafe strong magnets. It also has a pen and pencil holder in the sides so that you can take notes while using your phone.
- Also i have created a ring holder in the bottom base part of cad and now it can hold your rings too. 
- Also made sd card, sim ejector and a card holder in the base and sides of the holder. 
- Also you can add your secret notes by rolling it and inserting into the small hole in the front of it.
- similarly, i have provided a hollow structure in the bottom base of holder to which a metal block can be kept so that it doesnt feel light and have a good weight to hold a phone in it.

Lastly, there is a nail holder in the model too in the back so that you can attach your phone to a screw or nail mounted in wall.
